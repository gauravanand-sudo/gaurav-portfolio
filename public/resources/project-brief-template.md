# ga.tech Project Brief Template

## 1. Business outcome
What should be different after this project is complete?

## 2. Primary audience
Who will use or see the result?

## 3. Current problem
What is slow, broken, manual, confusing, or missing today?

## 4. Must-have first release
What is the smallest end-to-end flow that needs to work?

## 5. Nice-to-have later
What can wait until after launch?

## 6. Existing assets
Website, codebase, brand files, APIs, documents, data, analytics, examples.

## 7. Proof / trust assets
Testimonials, founder credentials, case studies, customer logos, numbers you can substantiate.

## 8. Integrations / constraints
Payments, auth, CRM, cloud, compliance, security, data residency, third-party systems.

## 9. Timeline
Target launch date and any immovable milestones.

## 10. Budget range
A rough range helps shape a realistic solution.

## 11. Ownership / handover
Who owns hosting, source code, design files, accounts and ongoing maintenance?

## 12. Success measure
How will you know the project worked?
